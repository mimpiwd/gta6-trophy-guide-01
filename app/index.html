⚠ The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot) is the only potentially missable trophy. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose Accept. It becomes unavailable after Chapter 1 — so do it before the chapter ends and keep a manual save from Chapter 1. Every other trophy can be earned after the story in free-roam.

      About this guide

      Written 19 November 2026, the day after Grand Theft Auto VI launched, in the author's own words. Names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list as carried by PSNProfiles and Exophase. The main guide source is the PowerPyx GTA 6 Trophy Guide & Roadmap (powerpyx.com, 18–19 Nov 2026); every trophy names its source beside its guide. Three trophies — In The Beginning…, Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin — were still unconfirmed by every guide on that date, and their guides say so honestly instead of inventing a method. This guide works fully offline: it loads nothing from the network, and it uses no Rockstar logo, artwork or game screenshots.
